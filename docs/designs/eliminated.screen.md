## Purpose
Tell the player who got them, then keep them watching.

## Elements
`OUT OF LIVES!` with three empty hearts, `by God Of War · 01:12 left`, stats so far (kills, blocks, eggs),
"Spectating God Of War in 3…", `Spectate now`, `Leave`.

## Behaviour
After 3 s switch to the Spectator HUD following the killer (or the leader if it was a self-blast).
